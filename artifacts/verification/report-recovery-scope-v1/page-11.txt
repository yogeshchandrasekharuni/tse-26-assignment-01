What the evidence cannot establish
A precise and reproducible workload still has boundaries. These limitations determine the claims we
can make, and the AI-use reflection distinguishes plausible authored material from observed
execution.
Threats to validity
Construct: observation counts are not unique homes; asking-price medians are not sale prices; first-to-last
change is not year-over-year change. Internal: common helpers can share faults; independent raw preflight and
hand-calculated tests reduce but do not eliminate this risk. Statistical: thirty overlapping designed slices, reuse
of successful sources and three fixed seed conditions are not independent representative Bernoulli trials.
Intervals are nominal; rankings mix model-contract behavior, transformations and the declared reuse policy.
External: a regular synthetic feed, selected ZIPs, one local model, one machine and a frozen period do not
characterize live housing markets or other agents. Playback samples no fresh behavior and does not increase
the sample size. Runtime comparisons are descriptive after the reboot and additional transport hop.
AI-use reflection
An LLM could draft the architecture, strict schemas, relation hypotheses, code scaffolding, diagrams, report
prose and plausible tests without running this system. The initial AI-generated handoff is substantive assistance
too. Those artifacts can look convincing while containing wrong API assumptions or invalid mathematics. The
observed evidence is different: exact generated bytes, real parent and specialist responses, a review changing
scan strategy, complete paired outcomes, recorded failures and a clean offline rerun must come from
execution. An LLM can calculate a Wilson interval; it cannot honestly invent the outcomes to which that interval
is applied. During implementation, an AI-chosen sparse band made price widening vacuous, which independent
input counts caught. Exact replay exposed reordered tool calls and repeated clone-name response selection. A
framework documentation assumption was checked against installed source rather than trusted from prose. An
AI-issued recovery command also ran after a failed preflight; it was stopped and its failed attempts retained.
AI_USE.md and NOTEBOOK.md record these mistakes and how they were caught. Authored test fixtures and
injected omissions remain labeled controls; they do not become empirical model findings. The interface also
keeps rounded display values separate from canonical statistics. Human authorship and submission remain the
team's responsibility; author identities have not been fabricated.
